New Game+

Locked, for now

Reach level 50, finally settle things with the Old Bastard™, and retire your hero to the Hall of Legends. That unlocks New Game+.

A New Game+ hero inherits a legacy from every race and class in the Hall: a bonus to their primary attribute and a smaller one to their secondary. There are 24 races and 24 classes; pick new pairings and it takes 24 heroes to collect them all, for +45% to every attribute.

A plain New Game always starts without a legacy, if you'd rather do it the hard way.

☠ Hardcore

One life. Every defeat has a small chance to be your hero's last: more while Wounded, more against tougher monsters, and much more against the Old Bastard™. When they fall, they stay fallen, and the Hall of the Fallen gets an obituary.

A Hardcore hero carries the legacy of everyone in your Hall of Legends (no legacy yet). With all 48 legacies, about 3 heroes in 10 reach level 50 alive; with none, about 1 in 50.

About Progress Quest Remix

This is a fork of the original Progress Quest that leans heavily into the parody. It is single-player and runs entirely in your browser; your characters are saved here, on this device.

It was not easy. We started with hardware emulation of an 80386-equipped PC clone running a novel Windows XP compatible operating system called ProgrOS, implemented in a clean room in the Seychelles by a team of people who had never programmed before. That had too much lag death, so we emulated a Cray-2 and ran the 80386 in that. The 80386 kept getting hot, so we switched to a 6502 overclocked at 120GHz with emulated liquid helium coolant. You're welcome.

The source code, issues and release notes live on GitHub.
